This is normally where a coach pays someone to write about them in the third person, so it sounds like a CV. I'd rather you just hear it from me.

Who am I? Sports Scientist, DCU. Inter-county footballer. Captain. I've been where you are: starting underage, fighting for a place, subbed off, dropped, injured and stuck on the sideline wondering if I'd get back to where I was.

To summarise it: I build the system that should already exist, then I hand you the record that proves it worked.

How It Works

The Dominant Athlete System.

You're putting in the work. That's not the problem. You're training hard, you've bought in, you're doing more than most lads on your team.

But you've hit a point where effort on its own stops moving the needle. You need the gym work to actually show up on the pitch, a structure that builds in order instead of a pile of sessions, and a record that proves it's working instead of hoping it is.

Here's what we see in almost every athlete who comes to us:

  • No priority. Trying to improve everything at once, so nothing gets enough attention to move.
  • No record. Nothing measured at the start, so nothing's proven at the end.
  • More isn't better. More sessions, more volume, more fatigue, never fresh for what matters.

If you're reading that thinking "that's me" … good. Use it as a mirror. The Dominant Athlete System is a complete training structure, built for any team-sport athlete, in any code, run through three phases, in strict order.

Phase 01 · You Leave Available

Robust Foundations

Before anything else, we fix how you move and build what takes the hits. Mechanics and mobility first, left-versus-right measured, not guessed.

Then tissue capacity: the muscle and tendon that absorb contact, eccentric work included, the best-evidenced injury reduction there is.

Then the engine under the engine, your aerobic base, because how fast you recover between efforts is decided here, not in season.

Phase 02 · You Leave Relentless

Powerful Engine

Heavy, well-coached strength to a real target, because every later output gets expressed from this number.

Then speed, agility and quickness: your first three steps, braking hard, cutting away from your marker.

Then repeat power, the actual demand of the game. High-intensity efforts, over and over, and you're still there in the last twenty minutes.

Phase 03 · You Leave Untouchable

Explosive Athleticism

Top-end speed, and how you use it in a match, because every sprint you make is a percentage of this ceiling.

Then reactivity: jumps, bounds, contrast work, so the strength you built gets into the ground instead of staying in the gym.

Then a planned peak and your own match-day routine, so you arrive ready instead of hoping.

Around all three phases sits the Performance Passport. Built week 0. Stamped week 12. Same tests, same protocol, both ends, so the numbers do the talking instead of me.

The frameworks are universal. The programme is built around you.
